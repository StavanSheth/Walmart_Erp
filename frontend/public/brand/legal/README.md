# Walmart Brand Identity Guidelines & Asset Compliance

This directory contains official brand asset mappings for the Walmart ERP Application Shell.

## Official Source
All brand assets, wordmarks, marks, and typography guidelines must strictly originate from the official [Walmart Brand Center](https://brandcenter.walmart.com/).

### Spark Guidelines
- The Walmart Spark is a registered trademark and the primary visual identifier.
- **Strict Rule**: The Spark must **never** be redrawn, rotated, distorted, recolored (other than official Walmart Yellow #FFC220 or pure White #FFFFFF on dark backgrounds), or synthesized through informal CSS/SVG approximations.
- Official reference: [Walmart Brand Center — Spark](https://brandcenter.walmart.com/brand/brand-identity/spark)

### Wordmark Guidelines
- The Walmart Wordmark is custom drawn artwork and must never be set in standard desktop typography.
- Official reference: [Walmart Brand Center — Wordmark](https://brandcenter.walmart.com/brand/brand-identity/wordmark)

### Typography: Everyday Sans
- Everyday Sans is Walmart's proprietary geometric typeface.
- Permitted font files should be placed in `frontend/public/brand/fonts/EverydaySans/`.
- Fallback font stack: `"Everyday Sans", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif`.
- Official reference: [Walmart Brand Center — Typography](https://brandcenter.walmart.com/brand/brand-identity/typography)

### Navigation Artwork (mobile-nav-bottom-right.webp)
- Located at `frontend/public/images/navigation/mobile-nav-bottom-right.webp`.
- User-supplied artwork slot for bottom-right mobile navigation dock.
